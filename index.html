Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Karmiq.

Is Karmiq free?

+

Yes! Karmiq offers a 100% Free Forever plan for small businesses with up to 3 staff members. You get full access to QR attendance, working hours, salary balance, and Aaj Ka Hisaab.

How does QR attendance work?

+

Employee receives a Karmiq ID card containing a unique QR code. The shop's attendance device scans it and marks attendance in under 1 second.

Does an employee need a smartphone?

+

No. Employee does NOT need a smartphone for QR attendance. They simply carry their printed or laminated Karmiq ID card.

How are working hours calculated?

+

Employee checks in once when arriving. Working time continuously calculates up to your shop's scheduled closing time. If staff takes a personal break, you can record an Out/Back entry to subtract that time. There is no manual checkout required.

Can I record partial salary payments?

+

Yes. You can record any number of advance payments in Cash, UPI, or Bank Transfer throughout the month. Karmiq automatically deducts each payment and updates the remaining balance.

Can I use Karmiq for a small shop?

+

Absolutely! Karmiq is specifically created for retail stores, kirana shops, salons, restaurants, workshops, garages, and small offices.

Can I add multiple employees?

+

Yes. You can add up to 3 staff on the Free plan, up to 25 staff on Pro, and up to 50 staff on Business.

Is voice assistant available?

+

The Voice Assistant is currently in active preview and marked as Coming Soon. You can try simulated voice queries in the app preview today!
